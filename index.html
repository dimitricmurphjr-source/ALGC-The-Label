DANELE.

Chosen. Equipped. Battle Ready.
Artist Story

Her story.
Her ministry.

Raised in Homestead, Texas, DANELE experienced a challenging childhood after her parents’ divorce and later found a strong foundation in God through her father. After struggling with drugs and alcohol in high school, she discovered her purpose through faith, eventually getting baptized and answering God’s call.

What started as making music for fun became a passion and a ministry. Today, she uses her real-life experiences and God-given lyrics to encourage others to stay Battle Ready—reminding them that they are chosen, equipped, and part of God’s army.

ALGC The Label Artist

BTheOne.

Born 2 BTheOne. Be who you are in Christ.
Artist Story

His story.
His purpose.

BTheOne is a Missouri City, Texas artist whose music is rooted in faith, purpose, and perseverance. Starting to rap at 16, he eventually felt God calling him away from secular music and toward using his gift to encourage others.

“Born 2 BTheOne” is more than a name—it’s a message to be who you are in Christ and embrace the uniqueness God placed inside you. Through his music, BTheOne encourages people to press through trials, embrace their purpose, and keep moving forward in faith.
